5. User Roles
Customer — Creates tasks, uploads documents, pays, approves access, tracks progress, confirms completion and reviews.
Verified Service Agent — Accepts assigned tasks, performs permitted assistance, updates status, uploads proof and communicates through the platform.
Professional — For services requiring specialized expertise, such as tax/accounting/legal/professional work, subject to applicable law and credentials.
Operations/Admin — Verifies agents, manages services, pricing, tasks, disputes, payouts, content and audit logs.
6. Agent Verification & Safety
Agent onboarding should include identity verification, phone verification, address/profile checks, skills/category verification, training, agreement and admin approval.
* Agent must never claim to be a government official unless they actually are and the representation is legally accurate.
* No bribery, forged documents, false information or guaranteed government approval.
* No password or OTP sharing/collection.
* No unauthorized signature or account access.
* Every task receives a unique Task ID and every material action is logged.
* Customer documents are shared only with task-scoped permission and access should expire after the task.

14. Pricing & Revenue Model
Customer-facing pricing should clearly separate government/third-party fees from platform service fees and any agent/physical-assistance charge.
* Basic information/checklist fee (optional)
* Application/document assistance fee
* Physical task/office assistance fee
* Business task packages
* Corporate subscription later
* Diaspora/family administration packages later
Example invoice: Government/official fee ?500 + Platform service fee ?300 + Physical assistance ?500 = Customer total ?1,300. Actual fees must be based on the applicable service and current official information.
15. AI Module
AI should help interpret customer language, identify likely services, generate checklists from a verified service database, explain next steps and route tasks. It should not invent government requirements or independently decide legal eligibility.
AI flow: User message ? Intent detection ? Service matching ? Verified service data ? Human/operations review when needed ? Customer response.

17. MVP Recommendation
Do not launch every service at once. Start with Dhaka and 3 service families that demonstrate the marketplace model: Passport assistance, academic certificate/document correction or collection, and Trade License assistance.
MVP goal: prove that customers will pay to outsource the task, agents can complete it reliably, and the platform can manage trust, documents, payment and status tracking.
Suggested MVP sequence: Landing page ? Customer task request ? Admin manually verifies request ? Agent assignment ? Document permission ? Task checklist/status ? Payment ? Proof ? Completion ? Review. Automate only after the manual workflow is proven.

Final Product Definition
This startup is not primarily a government-information portal. It is a trusted task-completion marketplace. The customer describes a real-world administrative task, the platform structures the job, verifies and assigns a suitable human agent, controls document access and payment, tracks execution, records proof, and closes the task.
The long-term vision is to become a “Life Admin OS” for Bangladesh: one place where individuals, families and businesses can outsource, organize and track repetitive administrative work.
